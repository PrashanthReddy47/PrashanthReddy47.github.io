About me

As a Geospatial and Remote Sensing Analyst with a wealth of experience gained through various employment and educational opportunities, I have developed and implemented new methodologies for classifying paddy rice fields using Sentinel-2 imagery, resulting in a 10% improvement in classification accuracy.

My expertise includes machine learning, satellite imagery analysis, and various forms of remote sensing.

I’m passionate about technology and love working with data.
