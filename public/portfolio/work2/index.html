Tableau Country Wise Top Player's Cricket Records

Tableau Dashbaord Link

This Tableau project is an interactive dashboard that allows users to explore country-wise top player’s cricket records combined Test, ODI, and T20I Batting Records with the most runs in their career.

The dashboard provides a visual representation of the data, allowing users to quickly identify the top players from each country and their batting records.

The dashboard also includes interactive filters to further refine the data and explore the records of specific players.
