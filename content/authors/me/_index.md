I am an independent researcher and geospatial data scientist specializing in agricultural remote sensing and machine learning. My work combines satellite imagery analysis with advanced computational techniques to address critical challenges in food security and environmental monitoring.

Currently affiliated with the University of Pavia's Telecommunications & Remote Sensing Laboratory, I have published peer-reviewed research achieving state-of-the-art accuracy in rice paddy mapping across large-scale agricultural landscapes. My research bridges the gap between theoretical remote sensing methods and practical applications in precision agriculture.

I am passionate about leveraging open-source satellite data and cloud computing platforms to develop scalable solutions for agricultural monitoring, particularly in regions with fragmented smallholder farming systems. My approach emphasizes phenology-driven classification, rigorous validation methodologies, and the development of interactive tools that make remote sensing insights accessible to diverse stakeholders.
