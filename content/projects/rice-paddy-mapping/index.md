Satellite-based Rabi rice paddy field mapping in Telangana, India. Published in Environmental Challenges (Elsevier, 2025). Achieved 93.3% overall accuracy mapping 732,345 hectares across 32 districts using phenology-driven Sentinel-2 classification on Google Earth Engine.
